NHS England A&E Performance Analysis

I wanted to know whether the NHS was keeping its constitutional promise to see A&E patients within 4 hours. I sourced three years of official NHS England government data, merged annual reports across 124 Trusts and 47 million patient visits, standardised the datasets, mapped every Trust to its NHS region, and wrote 10 SQL queries to interrogate performance from every angle. The finding was stark — not one Trust met the 95% constitutional target in any of the three years. Nearly 20 million patients waited longer than they legally should have. Even after NHS England quietly lowered the target to 78% in March 2024, 109 out of 124 Trusts still failed. My local Sunderland Trust performed at 55.07% — meaning nearly half of all local A&E patients are failed every day.

"Not one Trust out of 124 met the 95% four-hour constitutional target across any of the three years analysed. This is not a local problem. It is a national, systemic failure - hidden in plain sight across three years of government data."
